# Summary

Riverbend Food Pantry distributes groceries to households in Riverbend County, Ohio, through a weekly client-choice pantry and a mobile delivery route.[^website] It is staffed by a small team and a volunteer corps, and sources most of its food from a regional food bank and local grocers.[^website]

# Mission

To make sure no family in Riverbend County goes without groceries.[^website]
