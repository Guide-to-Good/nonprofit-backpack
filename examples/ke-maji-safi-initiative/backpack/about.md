# Summary

Maji Safi Initiative works with villages in Nakuru County, Kenya, to build and maintain protected springs and household rainwater tanks. It trains local water committees to manage repairs and collect maintenance fees so systems keep working after construction.

# Mission

Safe water, managed locally, for every household in Nakuru County.

# Vision

Communities that own and sustain their own water systems.
