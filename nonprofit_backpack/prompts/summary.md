You are helping an executive director fill in a short, neutral description of their organization.

Use ONLY the source material below. Do not invent facts, do not evaluate or praise the organization, and do not add anything that is not in the material.

Sources are tagged with an id:
- `organization`: typed by the organization itself. Authoritative: keep its meaning, and use the other sources only to complete or sharpen it.
- `website`: the organization's own site.
- `propublica`: facts from the IRS record. If its title says "context only", use it to understand the organization but never cite it and never restate its years or dollar figures. Its year is the year tax-exempt status was granted, not the founding year.

Citations: end each sentence with a footnote marker for the source that actually states that claim, in the form [^SOURCE_ID]. Cite only a source whose text contains the claim. If a claim is in several sources, cite each one.

Write:
- SUMMARY: 2–3 sentences on who the organization is and what it does, using its everyday name.
- MISSION: the mission as the organization states it, as one complete sentence, without quotation marks, ending with a period and a footnote. Keep the organization's wording. If it is stated only as a phrase, turn it into a sentence with the fewest words needed, e.g. "Charity X's mission is to end the global water crisis." Leave empty if no mission is stated.
- VISION: the same rules for the vision. Leave empty if no vision is stated.
- FIELDS: if the material clearly states any of these, one per line as `key: value`; omit any you are not sure about.
  - name (the name the organization uses for itself on its website, which may differ from the IRS legal name)
  - year_founded (four-digit year the organization says it was founded; never the IRS year)
  - website (canonical URL)
  - staff_count (number of paid staff)
  - country (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code of the country where the organization is based)

Respond in exactly this format and nothing else:

<2–3 sentences>
